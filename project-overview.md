# Project Overview: Hospital Management Application

## 1.Problems statement
Currently, people rely on various actors to make a doctor appointment (going to the GP for a referral and making a phone call to make an appointment in the case of the private medical sector), view their lab results and medical history (usually hard copies). These processes frequently lead to prolonged waiting times and unnecessary stress for patients due to the uncertainty regarding their health status. Besides patients, the medical staff also heavily rely on manual tasks for data processing. For example, each ward manually compiles inpatient meal lists which the head of the department then has to digitize. The same process applies to hospital admission records, which are first documented on paper and later being manually entered into a computer. This, of course, leads to double work and double waiting time for patients to receive their medical care.   

## 2. Project Objectives
The goal of this project is to design a hospital management application that can be used by healthcare professionals in both public and private hospitals, as well as by everyday patients. Key objectives include:
* **Eliminate** the need for physical documents in the healthcare systems
* **Provide** healthcare workers and everyday patients easier access to medical status
* **Shorten** the waiting period for healthcare services

## 3. Project Scope
### In Scope
* Healthcare professionals login using institutional credentials
* Insured/uninsured patients (with a health insurance card) login using their unique health insurance ID, card identification number and personal email
* Uninsured patients (without a health insurance card) login using national id and personal email
* Customized interfaces and tailored functionalities depending on the user's roles (healthcare worker/patient portal)
* Live status updates for appointments (pending, approved, rejected, completed, with instant access to test results and consultation summary)

### Out of Scope
* Native iOS and Android mobile applications (web-browser only initially)
* Patient Feedback section per consultation/ healthcare professional/ institution


## 4. Stakeholder Analysis
| Stakeholder | Role | Needs and Expectations|
| :--- | :--- | :---|
| **Patients** | Primary End Users | Want an easy to use web application to track their medical records|
| **Healthcare workers** | Primary End Users | Need an easy to use system to manage patient data |
| **Hospital Administrators** | Management and Operations | Need an easy was to manage staff, schedules, facility resources |
| **Hospital IT Department** | Technical Support | Needs the system to integrate safely with existing healthcare workers login credentials |
| **Public and Private Hospitals** | Institutional | Need the system to optimize their workflow |
| **National Health Insurance Institution** | Institutional | Expects the system to distinguish between insured and uninsured patients, to offer a clear digital trail of medical services, to reduce the administrative bottlenecks |

## 5. Success Metrics
* **Reduction in Administrative Data Entry Time**: 50-70% decrease in the time healthcare workers spend compiling and transcribing inpatient related information
* **Elimination of Double Work**: 80% of hospital workflows completed 100% digitally
* **Average Patient Waiting Time for Appointment Approved/Rejected**: 1 day
* **User Satisfaction Score**: users' stress levels and overall ease of use > 85%
* 
## 6. Risks
* User adoption: healthcare workers are accustomed to paper workflows and may hesitate to adopt a new digital tool
* Accessibility: elderly or non-tech-savvy people might struggle to adopt the system
* Integration and Compliance: lack of interoperability with the National Health Insurance Institution
